PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: July 2026

At Addyyo Studios, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how XOXO Neon Tic Tac Toe handles your information.

1. Data Collection

Our game is designed to work completely offline. We only store limited information locally on your device to improve your gameplay experience.

  • Game settings
  • Sound preferences
  • Selected neon themes
  • Temporary player names
  • Current match scores

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your privacy and do not collect, access, store, or share any personal information.

  • No name
  • No email address
  • No phone number
  • No contacts
  • No camera access
  • No microphone access
  • No photo gallery access
  • No GPS location
  • No advertising ID
  • No device identifiers

3. Internet Usage

XOXO Neon Tic Tac Toe does not require an internet connection. The game does not send or receive any information from external servers. All gameplay data remains on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking services, or external SDKs.

5. Data Deletion

All saved information is stored only on your device. You can permanently remove all stored data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Our game is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any future changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
